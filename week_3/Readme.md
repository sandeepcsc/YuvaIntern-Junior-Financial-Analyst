# 🧮 Valuation Methodologies

## 1️⃣ Discounted Cash Flow (DCF)

The DCF model estimates Tata Motors' intrinsic enterprise value by forecasting future **Unlevered Free Cash Flow (UFCF)** and discounting the cash flows using the **Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC)**.

### DCF Framework

```text
Revenue Forecast
       ↓
EBITDA Forecast
       ↓
EBIT Forecast
       ↓
NOPAT
       ↓
+ D&A
       ↓
- Capital Expenditure
       ↓
- Change in Working Capital
       ↓
Unlevered Free Cash Flow
       ↓
Discount Using WACC
       ↓
Enterprise Value
       ↓
Equity Value
       ↓
Implied Share Price
```



## 📈 2. Comparable Company Analysis

Comparable Company Analysis (CCA) was conducted to estimate Tata Motors' implied valuation by benchmarking it against selected publicly traded automotive companies. The objective of this method is to understand how the market values businesses with similar operating characteristics and to apply relevant market multiples to Tata Motors' FY2025–26 financial performance.

### 🔎 Selected Peer Group

The analysis considers the following listed automotive companies:

| Company | Ticker | FY26 EV / Sales | FY26 EV / EBITDA | Selection |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| Ashok Leyland | ASHOKLEY | 2.48x | 11.60x | ✅ Selected |
| Force Motors | FORCEMOT | 2.77x | 16.78x | ✅ Selected |
| Eicher Motors | EICHERMOT | 7.57x | 32.40x | ❌ Excluded |

Ashok Leyland and Force Motors were selected as the primary peer group because their business exposure provides a closer comparison with Tata Motors' commercial-vehicle operations. Eicher Motors has been retained as a market reference but excluded from the selected peer average because its business mix and profitability profile are materially different.

### 🧮 EV / Sales Valuation

The selected peer average EV/Sales multiple is **2.63x**.

Applying this multiple to Tata Motors' FY26 revenue of **₹83,855 crore** results in an implied enterprise value of approximately **₹2,20,119 crore**.

After incorporating the model's net-cash adjustment of approximately **₹2,082 crore**, the implied equity value is approximately **₹2,22,201 crore**.

This produces an implied value of approximately:

### 💰 ₹603.81 per share

### 📊 EV / EBITDA Valuation

The selected peer average EV/EBITDA multiple is **14.19x**.

Applying this multiple to Tata Motors' FY26 underlying EBITDA of **₹10,314 crore** results in an implied enterprise value of approximately **₹1,46,356 crore**.

After incorporating the net-cash adjustment, the implied equity value is approximately **₹1,48,438 crore**.

This produces an implied value of approximately:

### 💰 ₹403.36 per share

### ⚖️ Selected Trading Comparable Valuation

To create a balanced market-based valuation, equal weights have been assigned to the EV/Sales and EV/EBITDA approaches.

| Valuation Method | Implied Share Value | Weight | Weighted Value |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| EV / Sales | ₹603.81 | 50% | ₹301.90 |
| EV / EBITDA | ₹403.36 | 50% | ₹201.68 |
| **Selected Trading Comps Value** | **₹503.59** | **100%** | **₹503.59** |

### 🎯 Comparable Companies Implied Value: ₹503.59 per share

### 💡 Interpretation

The comparable-company analysis indicates that Tata Motors could command a higher valuation when benchmarked against selected commercial-vehicle peers, particularly under the EV/Sales methodology. The EV/EBITDA approach gives a more conservative result because it places greater emphasis on the company's current operating profitability.

The valuation range between **₹403.36 and ₹603.81 per share** demonstrates the sensitivity of market-based valuation to the choice of valuation multiple. This is an important consideration for a financial analyst because different multiples capture different dimensions of a company's performance.

EV/Sales focuses primarily on the scale of the business and the valuation assigned by the market to each rupee of revenue. EV/EBITDA, in contrast, reflects the market's assessment of operating profitability and cash-generating potential before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation.

The resulting blended comparable valuation of **₹503.59 per share** therefore provides a useful market-based benchmark alongside the DCF valuation. Rather than relying on a single methodology, the use of multiple market multiples creates a more balanced view of potential valuation.

### 📌 Analyst Note

Comparable-company analysis should be treated as a market-based benchmarking exercise rather than a standalone measure of intrinsic value. Differences in business mix, growth expectations, margins, capital intensity, leverage and investor sentiment can materially influence peer valuation multiples.
