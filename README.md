# 📈 Junior Financial Analyst Internship - YuvaIntern

> A repository showcasing the projects, financial models, and analytical reports completed during my 4-week virtual internship as a Junior Financial Analyst at YuvaIntern.

🗓️ **Duration:** August 27, 2026 – September 24, 2026

---

### 🚀 Weekly Projects Breakdown

📊 **Week 1: Financial Data Analysis & Reporting**
* Extracted and cleaned publicly available time-series corporate financial data.
* Calculated and analyzed key performance metrics, including growth rates, profit margins, and liquidity ratios.
* Visualized financial trends and authored a comprehensive corporate health report.

🏦 **Week 2: Financial Modeling for Investment Banking**
* Constructed a dynamic M&A (Mergers & Acquisitions) financial model from scratch in Excel.
* Developed forecasted income statements, balance sheets, and cash flow statements.
* Incorporated integration synergies and detailed sensitivity analyses on critical financial variables.

⚖️ **Week 3: Valuation Analysis & Investment Strategy Simulation**
* Performed advanced corporate valuations using **Discounted Cash Flow (DCF)** analysis.
* Conducted comparable company and precedent transaction analyses to establish fair market value.
* Drafted strategic investment recommendations based on simulated financial outcomes.

🛡️ **Week 4: Risk Management & Sensitivity Analysis**
* Built dynamic models to stress-test key inputs like interest rate fluctuations and revenue growth variations.
* Identified potential financial vulnerabilities in capital expansion scenarios.
* Formulated actionable risk mitigation strategies, focusing on hedging, diversification, and capital restructuring.
