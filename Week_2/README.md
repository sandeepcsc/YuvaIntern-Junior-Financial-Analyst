# Week 2 Task: Financial Modeling for Investment Banking

## 🎯 Objective
**Week 2 Objective**

* Build a comprehensive financial model that simulates a merger and acquisition scenario using Tata Motors' FY2025-26 financial data.
* Develop forecasted income statements, balance sheets, and cash flow statements using linked assumptions and scenario analysis.
* Evaluate alternative financing structures, transaction funding, integration synergies, liquidity requirements, and pro forma financial impact.
* Present the analysis in an investment-banking style Excel model supported by a professional M&A report.

## 📊 Project Highlights

**Financial Modeling & M&A Analysis**

* **Public Data Sourcing & Raw Data Structuring:** Tata Motors FY2025-26 consolidated income statement, balance sheet and cash flow statement data are extracted from the annual report and organized into a usable financial-model input structure.
* **Historical Financial Base:** FY26 consolidated revenue was ₹83,855 crore and profit for the year was ₹3,030 crore. Total assets were ₹52,309 crore and total equity was ₹12,734 crore at March 31, 2026.
* **Cash Flow Foundation:** FY26 net cash from operating activities was ₹14,981 crore, net cash used in investing activities was ₹3,451 crore, net cash used in financing activities was ₹5,223 crore, and closing cash and cash equivalents were ₹6,899 crore.
* **Forecast Financial Statements:** The model projects revenue, operating profitability, net income, working capital, capital expenditure, debt, cash, and equity over the forecast period using linked assumptions.
* **M&A Transaction Modeling:** The scenario models Tata Motors' proposed acquisition of Iveco Group. The annual report states an all-cash voluntary tender offer at €14.1 per share, representing approximately €3.8 billion / ₹41,691 crore of equity consideration as of March 31, 2026, excluding Iveco's Defence business. The proposed transaction remained subject to regulatory approvals.
* **Financing Scenarios:** The model evaluates debt, equity, and cash funding alternatives and traces their effects on interest expense, liquidity, leverage, and shareholder dilution.
* **Integration Synergies:** Synergy assumptions are phased over time to reflect operational integration, purchasing benefits, manufacturing efficiencies, cross-selling, and other potential value-creation opportunities.
* **Pro Forma Analysis:** Standalone Tata Motors forecasts are combined with the modeled acquisition contribution, transaction costs, financing effects, and synergies to produce pro forma financial outputs.
* **Sensitivity & Scenario Analysis:** Key transaction assumptions are stress-tested to show how purchase price, synergy realization, financing cost, and operating performance affect value creation and accretion/dilution.
* **Worked Examples & Evidence:** The workbook includes explicit calculations for acquisition funding, interest expense, synergy realization, EPS impact, liquidity requirements, and other key model outputs rather than relying only on summary figures.
* **Visual Trend Analysis:** Dashboard charts summarize revenue, EBITDA/profitability, cash generation, debt movement, acquisition contribution, and scenario outcomes.
* **Model Integrity & Controls:** Balance-sheet checks, sources-and-uses checks, debt/cash consistency checks, and minimum-liquidity checks are included to improve model reliability.
* **Actionable Limitations & Suggestions:** The model distinguishes reported historical data from forward-looking assumptions and identifies practical actions around financing, FX exposure, working capital, purchase price allocation, synergy tracking, integration governance, and liquidity protection.

## ⚠️ Modeling Notes

* Historical Tata Motors figures are source-derived from the FY2025-26 annual report.
* Forecast operating assumptions, synergy realization, financing mix, interest rates, working-capital assumptions, and other forward-looking inputs are model assumptions rather than reported historical facts.
* The Iveco transaction is treated as a proposed transaction in the model and should not be interpreted as completed unless separately confirmed by an authoritative transaction-closing announcement.
* FY2025 comparative figures in the Tata Motors report include a shorter comparative period in several sections and therefore should be interpreted carefully when calculating growth rates.
* All monetary values in the financial model are generally presented in ₹ crore unless otherwise stated.
